Projects

Robot Imitation Learning for Manipulation

A shared training pipeline for Flow Matching, Diffusion Policy, and IMLE. The policies take head-camera RGB, wrist-camera RGB, and optional joint state as input, then predict 16-step action chunks for seven arm joints and six hand targets.

The project covers data preprocessing, action-label conventions, policy training, checkpoint packaging, and Isaac-based rollout evaluation.

Training and evaluation details
  • Historical offline training runs are complete for Flow Matching, Diffusion Policy, and IMLE.
  • Episode-level splits, masked action padding, and explicit observation/action alignment are used across the training pipeline.
  • Video-assisted training and Joint WAM are experimental branches.
  • Selected successful rollouts are shown above. A method comparison requires the same initialization, action contract, and success criterion.

Industrial 6D Object Pose Estimation

A stereo-vision workflow covering dual-RGB calibration, ZED capture, depth validation, and data preparation for RGB-D pose estimation. FoundationStereo integration connects stereo matching with the downstream pose pipeline.

The work includes checking camera calibration and depth alignment before estimating an object’s position and orientation.
